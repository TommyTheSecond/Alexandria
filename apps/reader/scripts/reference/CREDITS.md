## The School of Athens (current main illustration)

Raphael, *The School of Athens*, 1511. Public-domain painting and faithful reproduction, as identified on the user-supplied file page:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:%22The_School_of_Athens%22_by_Raffaello_Sanzio_da_Urbino.jpg

Source JPEG: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/49/%22The_School_of_Athens%22_by_Raffaello_Sanzio_da_Urbino.jpg

The interface renders a static grayscale ASCII conversion; the original painting's geometry and aspect ratio are preserved. Reproduce with `powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/build-athens.ps1`.

Lady Justice: P3 supplied the engraving as Alexandria.png. The unchanged supplied copy is retained as lady-justice.png here; build-justice.ps1 samples it into a 96-column by 120-row grayscale ASCII asset. No external image source was fetched for this change.
